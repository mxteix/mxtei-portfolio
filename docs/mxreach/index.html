How it works

Four steps, then it runs itself.

Step 01

Build the list

Describe who you want to reach. mxReach finds them and verifies the contact.

Step 02

Write once

One message, personalised per contact. You approve the tone before anything sends.

Step 03

Send everywhere

Email first, social as backup. Follow-ups stop the moment someone replies.

Step 04

Close in one inbox

Every reply lands in one place, with a drafted answer waiting for you.
