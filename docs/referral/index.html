Referral program

Send someone my way, you both save.

Most of my work comes from people passing my name on. This just makes that worth something. They save 15% on their first invoice, and you get 15% back on your next one.

Get my code Read the terms

How it works

Four steps, no small print you’ll regret.

01

Get your code

Ask me for a referral code. It’s yours, tied to your name, and it doesn’t expire.

02

Pass it on

Give it to anyone who needs a site, an app, a bot or community work.

03

They save 15%

Their first invoice comes in 15% lower. They just mention the code before we agree scope.

04

You save 15%

You get 15% credit against your next invoice with me, once theirs is paid.

The terms

All of them, in plain English.

  • 15% off applies to the new client’s first invoice only, not to everything they ever book.
  • Your 15% credit lands once their first invoice is paid, and comes off your next invoice with me.
  • It applies to freelance service work billed hourly. It doesn’t apply to mxReach subscriptions.
  • The code has to be mentioned before we agree scope — I can’t apply it retroactively to a quote that’s already signed off.
  • One referral discount per new client. Codes can’t be stacked with each other.
  • Credit only — there are no cash payouts.
  • Codes are issued and applied by hand right now, so there’s nothing to sign up for and nothing tracking you.
